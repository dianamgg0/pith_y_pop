Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

PithyPop respects the privacy of its users, especially within a child-focused educational environment. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and users’ rights.

1. Information We Collect

PithyPop is designed to minimize the collection of personal data.

  • Anonymous technical data: device type, browser, language, and visited pages.
  • Usage data: interaction with educational content.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as names, addresses, or email addresses from children.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Improve the educational experience
  • Optimize site performance
  • Understand how users interact with content

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

3. Cookies

We may use cookies to analyze traffic and improve navigation. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

PithyPop may use third-party services such as analytics tools or app distribution platforms. These services handle data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

PithyPop is designed as an educational tool for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that such data has been collected, they may contact us to request its removal.

6. Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access.

7. External Links

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites linked from our site.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

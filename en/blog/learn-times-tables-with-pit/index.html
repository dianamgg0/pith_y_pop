Meet Pit

Pit is an adventurous, curious, and energetic frog who loves learning through play.

His favorite hobby is numbers, and his mission is to help children practice times tables in a fun and motivating way.

Learn Math in a Space Adventure

Pit lives in a magical world full of colors, surprises, and exciting challenges where numbers come to life.

Throughout the adventure, players solve multiplication tasks that strengthen:

  • Memory
  • Mental speed
  • Focus
  • Number fluency
  • Confidence in math

Pit's Magic Pop-it

Pit uses a special Pop-it toy that helps him focus while popping bubbles filled with numbers.

Each completed challenge rewards progress and keeps children motivated to continue learning.

Earn Pitcoins and Customize Pit

Every correct answer and completed level gives players Pitcoins, the currency of Pit’s world.

They can be used to unlock fun items such as:

  • Hats
  • Sunglasses
  • Special accessories
  • Pit's favorite food

This turns learning into a rewarding experience with goals and progress.

Take Care of Your Virtual Pet

Pit also needs daily care. In the magical forest, players help him catch flies, avoid poisonous mushrooms, and stay healthy.

  • Give him a bath
  • Brush his teeth
  • Feed him
  • Protect him on adventures

Why This Method Works

When children connect math with play, rewards, and positive emotions, they practice more consistently.

The combination of a virtual pet, progression systems, and multiplication challenges makes learning feel natural and exciting.

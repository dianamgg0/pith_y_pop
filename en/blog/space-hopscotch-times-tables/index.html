Learn Math in Space

Space is not only exciting, it is also the perfect setting to practice times tables before beginning the great adventure with Pit.

In this universe full of stars, each space base was designed to help children strengthen their math skills through play.

Practice Before Takeoff

Before starting the main mission, you can train in different space bases.

Each base shows multiplication results as repeated addition, helping children understand how every answer is formed step by step.

  • See number patterns
  • Understand multiplication
  • Improve math memory
  • Learn through positive repetition

Space Hopscotch Challenges

As you move forward, you will find different platforms to jump between bases.

Each jump is a decision that tests your memory and mental speed.

If you choose the wrong base, you travel backward through space time and must try again until you get the right answer.

A Fun Way to Memorize

The jumping gameplay turns traditional practice into an active and entertaining experience.

  • Learning through movement
  • Better concentration
  • Constant motivation
  • Repetition without boredom

Prepare for Bigger Challenges

Completing each space base strengthens your skills for bigger challenges inside the game.

Every correct answer builds confidence and brings you closer to becoming an expert in the math universe.
